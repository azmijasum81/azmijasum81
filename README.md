### About Me

I work across a mix of **Business Analysis and Project Management**, where my day-to-day role involves understanding business requirements, coordinating stakeholders, analysing business processes, and supporting projects from planning through execution.

Outside of my regular working hours, I enjoy practising **data analytics as a hobby**. I usually source dummy and publicly available datasets from **Kaggle** and use them as practical exercises to strengthen my analytical thinking and technical skills.

I enjoy exploring the Python ecosystem, particularly **Pandas** for data manipulation and **Streamlit** for building interactive analytical applications. For database work, I use **PostgreSQL** for structured data management, while **DuckDB** is one of my go-to tools for quickly exploring larger datasets and generating insights.

My goal is not simply to learn different tools, but to practise the **end-to-end data analytics process**:

**Business Question → Data Understanding → Data Cleaning → Exploratory Analysis → SQL / Analysis → Visualisation → Insights → Recommendations**

I enjoy learning by building, experimenting with different tools, and turning datasets into practical business insights.
